Common questions

How do live online classes work?

Classes are live video lessons. Before each class you'll get an email with the join link and a class code. You can also join from the Live classes section on this page by entering the code.

What happens if a class is cancelled or moved?

You'll get an email as soon as it changes, and the schedule on this page updates to show the new time or the cancellation.

Is the tutor licensed?

Yes. Classes are taught by a well-experienced tutor licensed by MOHRE, work permit no. MB323795674AE.

What does my child need for online classes?

A laptop, tablet or phone with a stable internet connection, a quiet place to sit, and ideally headphones.

Do you offer in-person classes near me?

In-person classes are available depending on your location. Message us with your area and we'll let you know.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the grade, subject, and whether you choose one-to-one or small-group, in-person or online classes. Call or message us for details.
